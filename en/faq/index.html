FAQ

Questions Importers and Buyers Actually Ask

Straight answers about our factory, the rotating skewer system, ordering terms and safe use. Everything below is what we tell a buyer on a first call - no marketing wording.

Factory, OEM and ordering

Who manufactures automatic rotating charcoal BBQ grills in China?

Blue Carbon manufactures automatic rotating charcoal BBQ grills at its integrated industry-and-trade factory in Yuhuan, Taizhou, Zhejiang, with a second production base in Qingdao, Shandong. The rotating skewer system is patented in China. We supply importers, distributors, retailers and brand owners under OEM and ODM programs, with a 50-piece minimum order per model.

Where can I find a smokeless BBQ factory that does OEM and private label?

Blue Carbon is a smokeless BBQ grill factory in Zhejiang, China, offering full OEM and private label service: your logo on the body, custom colour, retail packaging, user manual, and voltage and plug matched to the destination market. Development starts from your drawing or from an existing model, with samples before container production.

What is the minimum order quantity for a first order?

The MOQ is 50 pieces per model, which lets a new importer or distributor test the market without committing to a full container on the first order. Mixing several models in one container can be discussed for repeat orders.

Can I order one sample charcoal grill before committing to a container?

Yes. Samples are available on request so you can check the build quality, the motor, the finish and the cooking result before placing a container order. Sample cost is normally credited against the first bulk order.

Can I order the household grill in a custom colour such as gold, and what is the MOQ for custom colours?

Colour customisation is available on the FM household model only — the commercial MAX 20-skewer and PRO 40-skewer units are currently not available in custom colours and are supplied in stainless steel natural finish only. Gold is a custom colour, and other colours such as black or white are handled the same way; we can match a colour swatch before production. Custom colours start at 300 pieces, and because they are priced differently from the natural stainless finish they are quoted separately by colour and quantity, with the final price confirmed on the proforma invoice. Unless a colour is stated in the contract, all grills ship in stainless steel natural finish, and standard natural-finish orders keep the usual 50-piece-per-model MOQ.

How the grill works

What is a rotating skewer BBQ actually called?

In English it is usually called a rotating skewer grill, rotisserie skewer grill or automatic rotating kebab grill. In German the common terms are Schaschlikgrill and Grill mit Drehspiess, and in Dutch spiesenbarbecue or barbecue met draaispit. All of them describe the same thing: skewers turned by a motor so the food cooks without being flipped by hand.

How does a smokeless charcoal grill actually work?

Our grills use a removable independent chamber that separates the charcoal from the dripping fat, so grease does not fall onto the coals and burn. Burning grease is where most of the visible smoke comes from. The result is about 95 percent less visible smoke than a conventional open-fire charcoal grill. It is nearly smokeless, not smoke-free, and it still burns real charcoal.

Is a smokeless charcoal grill completely smoke-free?

No, and no charcoal grill can be. Our design cuts visible smoke by about 95 percent compared with an open-fire grill, which is why we describe it as nearly smokeless. Because it burns charcoal it must always be used outdoors or in a well-ventilated area.

Does a rotating charcoal grill need mains electricity?

No. The WQ157 drive motor runs on a USB power bank or on two D-size (R20) batteries, so the grill works on a balcony, in a garden or while camping with no socket nearby. A CE-marked mains adapter for 220-240 V, 50 Hz is supplied for UK and EU use.

Hygiene and cleaning

Does the grill come with a lid, and why does that matter for food hygiene?

Yes. The MAX and PRO can be supplied with a protective metal lid at no extra cost. The lid sits over the skewers while they cook, so dust, insects and - the part people forget at a buffet counter - droplets from anyone talking, laughing or coughing nearby never land on the food. Staff do not have to work over an open grill wearing a mask, and what reaches the plate is cleaner than on an open mangal. The lid also holds heat around the load, so a full rack cooks a little faster.

Does the FM come with a lid?

Yes. The FM is supplied with a metal lid welded to the body at the factory - it is standard, not an optional extra. The lid covers the skewers while they cook, so dust, insects and droplets from anyone talking nearby stay off the food, and it holds heat around the load. On the MAX and PRO a protective metal lid is available as well, there at no extra cost.

Choosing the right model

What size charcoal grill do I need for a 60-cover restaurant?

The PRO, in the 40 or 60 skewer configuration. For a 60-cover service one 60-skewer unit covers a full round of skewers at once. Kitchens running continuous service usually start with one PRO and add a second unit as volume grows.

What is the best charcoal grill for a restaurant serving 40 to 60 skewers at once?

The PRO, supplied in 40 and 60 skewer configurations and built in food-grade stainless steel for commercial service. It cooks a full load with no manual flipping and is designed for high-volume catering and buffet service.

Is a rotating skewer grill better than a flat skewer grill?

For volume, yes. On a flat grill someone has to turn every skewer by hand, and in a busy service that means uneven cooking and burnt edges. A rotating rack turns all of them at constant speed, so a full load cooks evenly and one person can run more than one unit.

Can I use a smokeless charcoal grill on a small balcony?

Yes, in most cases. The FM holds 12 skewers, weighs 8 kg and is small enough for a balcony, patio or tabletop. Check your building or landlord rules first, keep the grill clear of walls, railings and curtains, and never leave it unattended. It burns charcoal, so it needs open air.

Can you supply a larger grill, for example 80, 100, 120 or 140 skewers?

Yes. 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 and 140 skewer configurations are available, and the larger sizes are built by joining modules side by side. A full load still cooks in the same 5 to 6 minutes whatever the size - although the food itself decides the time, so thicker cuts or bone-in pieces need longer. Sizes come in multiples of the 20-skewer module, so odd counts such as 25, 30 or 35 skewers are not available. The trade-off is weight: a 140-skewer unit weighs roughly 115 kg, needs a solid floor and we recommend a dedicated load-bearing stand or frame (weight figures are approximate and confirmed per configuration). Price follows the configuration.

I need to serve 100 skewers at once in a busy restaurant - can one grill do that?

Yes. Twenty guests ordering five skewers each is 100 skewers, and a 100-skewer unit sends that whole order out in one pass instead of in rounds - which is what separates a kitchen that keeps up at peak from one that does not. It is the same rotating system as the smaller models: the motor turns every skewer at constant speed, a full load cooks in about 5 to 6 minutes, and how long exactly depends on the food - thicker cuts or bone-in pieces take longer. The required size follows the order: 40 or 60 skewers for a normal service, 80 to 140 skewers when several large tables order at the same time.

Safe use

Can I use a smokeless charcoal grill indoors?

No. These grills burn charcoal and produce carbon monoxide, a colourless and odourless gas that can be fatal. Use the grill only outdoors or in a well-ventilated area, never in a closed room, tent, caravan or basement, and never to heat a living space. An open window is not enough ventilation.

Is it safe to grill indoors with charcoal if the window is open?

No. Burning charcoal produces carbon monoxide, which has no colour or smell and can kill, and an open window does not remove the risk. Use the grill outdoors on a balcony or in a garden, or in a space with real mechanical ventilation, never in a closed room, tent or caravan.
