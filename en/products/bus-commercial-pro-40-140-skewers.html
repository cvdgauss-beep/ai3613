Overview

Product Description

BUS Commercial PRO ‑ Automatic Rotating Smokeless BBQ Grill

Equipped with an external replaceable drive motor, model WQ157, that rotates the skewers at constant speed. The motor is powered by a USB power bank only and can be swapped within 10‑15 seconds.

Food is heated evenly across the full skewer load with no manual flipping required, for hassle‑free and nearly‑smokeless barbecue performance. Cooking time depends on the food: around 5‑6 minutes for a full load of standard meat skewers, longer for thicker cuts, bone‑in pieces or larger vegetables. This model is supplied in 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 and 140 skewer configurations, and the larger sizes are built by joining modules side by side, so a full load still cooks in the same 5‑6 minutes.

Because a standard cycle takes about 5‑6 minutes, multi-round events can use fewer units than a one-round estimate. Confirm the required quantity against the event duration, loading and unloading workflow, and acceptable waiting time.

The body, grates, oil tray and skewers use 304 stainless steel for durability and straightforward cleaning. This model can be supplied with a protective metal lid at no extra cost: it keeps dust, insects and droplets from people talking nearby off the food while it cooks, and it holds heat around the load.

Designed for high-volume catering and buffet service, this charcoal grill is supplied in 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 or 140 skewer configurations. A 20-skewer commercial unit is the base module, so larger configurations add capacity across joined modules; actual throughput still depends on the menu, loading workflow and service window. The trade-off is weight, and the largest units need a fixed position. Charcoal is used as fuel, so the unit must never be operated in a closed room and must be kept clear of curtains, upholstery and other flammable surfaces. A 140-skewer unit weighs roughly 115 kg, so it needs a solid floor and we recommend a dedicated load-bearing stand or frame; weight figures are approximate and confirmed per configuration. Sizes are built in multiples of the 20-skewer module (40, 60, 80, 100, 120, 140), so odd counts such as 25, 30 or 35 skewers are not available.

Full OEM and ODM service is available for this model, including custom branding, colour, packaging and manual options, subject to quotation.

Images on this page show the version without the lid. A protective metal lid can be added at no extra cost. Skewer count, dimensions and packing differ by model.

Commercial Buyer Questions

Direct answers about capacity, outdoor operation, power, cleaning, spare parts and order configuration. Final specifications and commercial terms are confirmed in the quotation and PI.

How do I estimate capacity for a 60-cover restaurant?

Calculate expected skewers from covers multiplied by typical skewers per guest, then divide the service into acceptable rounds. The PRO 40 or 60 configuration may be a starting point, but the correct choice depends on the service window, food type, loading workflow and waiting time. Confirm the model against your own numbers.

How do I cook 50 skewers at the same time without burning them?

Load the 60-skewer PRO and let the motor rotate them at constant speed. The rotation is what prevents burning: every skewer passes the same heat in turn instead of sitting still over the hottest spot, so no one has to stand over the grill flipping them.

Which charcoal grill fits a restaurant serving 40 to 60 skewers at once?

The PRO, supplied in 40 and 60 skewer configurations and built in 304 stainless steel for commercial service. It cooks a full load with no manual flipping and is designed for high-volume catering and buffet service.

How does a rotating skewer grill compare with a flat skewer grill?

For volume service, the rotating design reduces manual turning. On a flat grill someone has to turn every skewer by hand, and in a busy service that can lead to uneven cooking and burnt edges. A rotating rack turns all of them at constant speed, so one person can operate more than one unit.

What grill do Turkish and Middle Eastern restaurants use for charcoal shish?

Traditionally a mangal or ocakbasi, a flat charcoal grill with skewers laid across it. Many kitchens now use an automatic rotating charcoal grill instead, because it cooks the same shish and kebab without a cook standing over the coals turning skewers all service.

Can the commercial grill be used inside a restaurant?

Do not install or operate this charcoal grill in an enclosed indoor kitchen. It must be used outdoors or in a genuinely well-ventilated area. Any commercial installation must be reviewed against local fire, ventilation and insurance requirements by the buyer and qualified local professionals before purchase.
