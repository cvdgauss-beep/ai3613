Choose the Right Commercial Charcoal Grill

Start from the buying job: model capacity, application, site conditions and supply program. These checks help restaurant, catering, wholesale and import buyers compare the range without treating capacity as a guaranteed output promise.

Choose by skewer capacity

MAX holds up to 20 skewers. PRO is supplied in modular 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 and 140-skewer configurations.

Restaurant and catering use

Compare peak service, serving rounds, loading, cleaning, floor space and outdoor ventilation before selecting a commercial model.

Low-smoke outdoor design

The separate chamber is intended to reduce visible smoke from dripping fat. The grill uses real charcoal, is not smoke-free and must not be used indoors.
