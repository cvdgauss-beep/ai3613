Overview

Product Description

FM Household Mini ‑ Automatic Rotating Smokeless BBQ Grill

Equipped with an external replaceable drive motor, model WQ157, that rotates the skewers at constant speed. The motor supports 3 power supply options and can be swapped within 3 seconds.

Food is heated evenly with no manual flipping required, for hassle‑free and nearly‑smokeless barbecue performance. Cooking time depends on the food: around 5‑6 minutes for a full load of standard meat skewers, longer for thicker cuts, bone‑in pieces or larger vegetables.

The whole unit is built of food‑grade stainless steel for high durability. Grease and stains can be wiped off easily for straightforward post‑use cleaning. This model comes with a welded metal lid as standard, fixed to the body at the factory.

Sized for balcony, patio, backyard, garden, apartment and camping use, this portable charcoal grill is compact and lightweight at 8 kg, and small enough to double as a tabletop grill. The skewers rotate like a rotisserie, so there is no need to stand over it flipping food as you would with a classic kettle grill - and the smokeless design keeps smoke down so the whole family can sit around it. It handles classic skewers just as well as shish kebabs, kabobs, meat, seafood and vegetables. Charcoal is used as fuel, so the unit must never be operated in a closed room and must be kept clear of curtains, upholstery and other flammable surfaces.

Full OEM and ODM service is available for this model, including voltage adjustment, plug selection and custom branding.
