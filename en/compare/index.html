How does a smokeless charcoal grill actually work?

Our grills use a removable independent chamber that separates the charcoal from the dripping fat, so grease does not fall onto the coals and burn. Burning grease is where most of the visible smoke comes from. The result is about 95 percent less visible smoke than a conventional open-fire charcoal grill. It is nearly smokeless, not smoke-free, and it still burns real charcoal.

Is a smokeless charcoal grill completely smoke-free?

No, and no charcoal grill can be. Our design cuts visible smoke by about 95 percent compared with an open-fire grill, which is why we describe it as nearly smokeless. Because it burns charcoal it must always be used outdoors or in a well-ventilated area.

Is a rotating skewer grill better than a flat skewer grill?

For volume, yes. On a flat grill someone has to turn every skewer by hand, and in a busy service that means uneven cooking and burnt edges. A rotating rack turns all of them at constant speed, so a full load cooks evenly and one person can run more than one unit.

Does the grill come with a lid, and why does that matter for food hygiene?

Yes. The MAX and PRO can be supplied with a protective metal lid at no extra cost. The lid sits over the skewers while they cook, so dust, insects and - the part people forget at a buffet counter - droplets from anyone talking, laughing or coughing nearby never land on the food. Staff do not have to work over an open grill wearing a mask, and what reaches the plate is cleaner than on an open mangal. The lid also holds heat around the load, so a full rack cooks a little faster.
