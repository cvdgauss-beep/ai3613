About us

Charcoal Grill Manufacturer & Factory

Integrated manufacturing-trading factory for automatic rotating low-smoke barbecue equipment, working with importers, distributors, retailers, and overseas agents.

Company profile

Manufacturing and export support for automatic rotating charcoal grills.

We are an integrated industry-trade enterprise located at No.46 Tianyou Road, Shamen Bingang Industrial City, Yuhuan City, Taizhou, Zhejiang Province, China. We also own one additional manufacturing base in Qingdao, Shandong Province.

Our engineering work focuses on repeated design, usability and production optimization for automatic rotating charcoal grills.

Our goal is to supply low-smoke barbecue solutions to customers worldwide. People can enjoy convenient outdoor cooking with real charcoal on a patio, terrace, in a garden or at a campsite.

You are warmly welcome to visit our factory for on‑site inspection. Global agents are currently being recruited.

  • Product Development: Model selection, colour, OEM branding, user manual and packaging solution.
  • Export Procedure: Inquiry handling, quotation, sample confirmation, PI issuance, production, inspection and shipment.
  • Order Terms Review: Payment terms, trade terms, lead‑time and document risk confirmation.
  • After‑sales Support: Spare‑parts supply, warranty service and distributor technical guidance.
