About us

A Manufacturing Partner for Export-Ready Smokeless Grills

Integrated manufacturing-trading factory for smokeless barbecue equipment patented in China, working with importers, distributors, retailers, and overseas agents.

Company profile

Technology-driven healthy smokeless barbecue solutions.

We are an integrated industry-trade enterprise located at No.46 Tianyou Road, Shamen Bingang Industrial City, Yuhuan City, Taizhou, Zhejiang Province, China. We also own one additional manufacturing base in Qingdao, Shandong Province.

After more than three years of repeated R&D and optimization, our smokeless barbecue grill is patented in China.

Our goal is to bring smokeless barbecue solutions to customers worldwide. People can enjoy convenient and delicious smokeless barbecue outdoors - on a balcony, a terrace, in a garden or at a campsite.

You are warmly welcome to visit our factory for on‑site inspection. Global agents are currently being recruited.

  • Product Development: Model selection, voltage customization, color‑option, OEM branding, user manual & packaging solution.
  • Export Procedure: Inquiry handling, quotation, sample confirmation, PI issuance, production, inspection and shipment.
  • Order Terms Review: Payment terms, trade terms, lead‑time and document risk confirmation.
  • After‑sales Support: Spare‑parts supply, warranty service and distributor technical guidance.
