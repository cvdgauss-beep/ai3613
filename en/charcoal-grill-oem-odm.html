OEM vs ODM: What's the Difference?

OEM starts with the buyer's brand and agreed requirements applied to a suitable product platform. The manufacturer produces the agreed configuration and branding.

ODM starts from an existing manufacturer product platform. The buyer requests agreed customisation or branding on that platform.

Blue Carbon's current OEM customization scope is based on existing product models and configurations. A new product platform or a wider development request requires separate written confirmation before it can be included in a quotation.

What Can Be Discussed for a Custom Charcoal Grill?

The programme can cover:

  • Product model and skewer configuration.
  • MAX 20-skewer or PRO 40-140-skewer range selection.
  • Branding, logo and panel requirements.
  • Custom colour, subject to a 200-unit MOQ.
  • Retail packaging and private-label cartons.
  • User manual and language requirements.
  • Material or configuration options confirmed in the quotation.
  • Export packing and shipment planning.

Logo, manual and packaging customization starts at 50 units with a custom fee. OEM/ODM minimum order is 100 units. Custom colour has a 200-unit minimum. All customization is subject to the exact model, written quotation and PI.

OEM / ODM Buying Process

  1. Send your product and market requirements.
  2. Confirm the product model and skewer configuration.
  3. Discuss branding, colour, packaging and manual requirements.
  4. Confirm target quantity and commercial terms.
  5. Approve the sample or specification when required.
  6. Start production after the agreed deposit.
  7. Complete the agreed quality inspection.
  8. Arrange export documents and shipment.

The standard lead time starts from 50 days after confirmed sample and deposit. Shipping time is additional. Sample fees and freight are paid by the customer; any sample-fee credit must be confirmed in the quotation.

Factory-Direct B2B Supply

Blue Carbon is positioned as an industry-and-trade manufacturer of automatic rotating charcoal grills based in Yuhuan, Zhejiang. The product range is offered to overseas importers, distributors, retail programs and equipment brands.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is charcoal grill OEM?

OEM means the buyer's brand and agreed requirements are applied to a suitable charcoal grill platform and production programme.

What is charcoal grill ODM?

ODM starts from an existing manufacturer product platform and adds agreed customization or branding. It does not automatically include a completely new product platform.

Can I order private-label charcoal grills?

Yes. Private-label branding, packaging and manual requirements can be discussed for the existing product range, subject to the quotation and PI.

Can you customize the product?

The current customization scope is based on existing Blue Carbon models and configurations. Logo, manual and packaging customization starts at 50 units with a custom fee. Custom colour has a 200-unit MOQ. OEM/ODM MOQ is 100 units.

What information should I provide for an OEM quote?

Provide target market, buyer type, model, capacity, quantity, branding, colour, packaging, manual language, destination, documentation and timeline.

What skewer capacities are available?

MAX holds up to 20 skewers. PRO is supplied in 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 and 140-skewer configurations.

Can you supply commercial quantities?

Yes. OEM/ODM production and commercial supply can be reviewed from the published MOQ and exact configuration. Final terms are confirmed in the quotation and PI.

How do I request an OEM quotation?

Send your product requirements and target market to the Blue Carbon export team. Include the information checklist above so the configuration and commercial scope can be reviewed.
