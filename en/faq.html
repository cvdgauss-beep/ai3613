How the grill works

What is a rotating skewer BBQ actually called?

In English it is usually called a rotating skewer grill, rotisserie skewer grill or automatic rotating kebab grill. In German the common terms are Schaschlikgrill and Grill mit Drehspiess, and in Dutch spiesenbarbecue or barbecue met draaispit. All of them describe the same thing: skewers turned by a motor so the food cooks without being flipped by hand.

How does a smokeless charcoal grill actually work?

Our grills use a removable independent chamber that separates the charcoal from the dripping fat, so grease does not fall onto the coals and burn. Burning grease is where most of the visible smoke comes from. The result is less visible smoke than a conventional open-fire charcoal grill. It is nearly smokeless, not smoke-free, and it still burns real charcoal.

Is a smokeless charcoal grill completely smoke-free?

No, and no charcoal grill can be. The separate-chamber design is intended to reduce visible smoke, but it is not smoke-free. Because it burns charcoal it must always be used outdoors or in a well-ventilated area.

Does a rotating charcoal grill need mains electricity?

No mains supply is required. The FM has 2 power-supply options: a USB power bank or two D-size (R20) batteries. MAX and PRO use a USB power bank only.

What are the main types of commercial kebab grills?

The main categories are flat charcoal grills, rotating charcoal skewer grills, gas skewer grills and electric skewer grills. Flat and rotating charcoal models give charcoal flavour but need outdoor or genuinely well-ventilated siting, while gas and electric units need a fixed gas or mains supply. Blue Carbon builds the rotating charcoal type.

How does a rotating charcoal grill compare with an electric skewer grill?

A rotating charcoal grill cooks over real charcoal, needs outdoor or genuinely well-ventilated siting, and its motor runs on battery power: the FM can use D-size batteries or a power bank, while MAX and PRO use a USB power bank only. An electric skewer grill needs a fixed mains supply and gives no charcoal flavour. Choose charcoal where flavour and placement flexibility matter, electricity where a socket and no-charcoal operation matter.

Hygiene and cleaning

Does the grill come with a lid, and why does that matter for food hygiene?

Yes. The MAX and PRO can be supplied with a protective metal lid at no extra cost. The lid sits over the skewers while they cook, so dust, insects and - the part people forget at a buffet counter - droplets from anyone talking, laughing or coughing nearby never land on the food. Staff do not have to work over an open grill wearing a mask, and what reaches the plate is cleaner than on an open mangal. The lid also holds heat around the load, so a full rack cooks a little faster.

Does the FM come with a lid?

Yes. The FM is supplied with a metal lid welded to the body at the factory - it is standard, not an optional extra. The lid covers the skewers while they cook, so dust, insects and droplets from anyone talking nearby stay off the food, and it holds heat around the load. On the MAX and PRO a protective metal lid is available as well, there at no extra cost.

Choosing the right model

How do I estimate capacity for a 60-cover restaurant?

Calculate expected skewers from covers multiplied by typical skewers per guest, then divide the service into acceptable rounds. The PRO 40 or 60 configuration may be a starting point, but the correct choice depends on the service window, food type, loading workflow and waiting time. Confirm the model against your own numbers.

Which charcoal grill fits a restaurant serving 40 to 60 skewers at once?

The PRO, supplied in 40 and 60 skewer configurations and built in 304 stainless steel for commercial service. It cooks a full load with no manual flipping and is designed for high-volume catering and buffet service.

How does a rotating skewer grill compare with a flat skewer grill?

For volume service, the rotating design reduces manual turning. On a flat grill someone has to turn every skewer by hand, and in a busy service that can lead to uneven cooking and burnt edges. A rotating rack turns all of them at constant speed, so one person can operate more than one unit.

Can I use a smokeless charcoal grill on a small patio?

Yes, in most cases. The FM holds 12 skewers, weighs 8 kg and is small enough for a patio or tabletop. Check your building or landlord rules first, keep the grill clear of walls, railings and curtains, and never leave it unattended. It burns charcoal, so it needs open air.

Can you supply a larger grill, for example 80, 100, 120 or 140 skewers?

Yes. 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 and 140 skewer configurations are available, and the larger sizes are built by joining modules side by side. A full load still cooks in the same 5 to 6 minutes whatever the size - although the food itself decides the time, so thicker cuts or bone-in pieces need longer. Sizes come in multiples of the 20-skewer module, so odd counts such as 25, 30 or 35 skewers are not available. The trade-off is weight: larger configurations are heavier and need a solid floor; we recommend a dedicated load-bearing stand or frame. The exact weight is confirmed per configuration in the quotation. Price follows the configuration.

I need to serve 100 skewers at once in a busy restaurant - can one grill do that?

Yes. Twenty guests ordering five skewers each is 100 skewers, and a 100-skewer unit sends that whole order out in one pass instead of in rounds - which is what separates a kitchen that keeps up at peak from one that does not. It is the same rotating system as the smaller models: the motor turns every skewer at constant speed, a full load cooks in about 5 to 6 minutes, and how long exactly depends on the food - thicker cuts or bone-in pieces take longer. The required size follows the order: 40 or 60 skewers for a normal service, 80 to 140 skewers when several large tables order at the same time.

How do I choose a commercial charcoal skewer grill?

Match the skewer count to your busiest service, then check how the unit is ventilated, powered and maintained. Blue Carbon offers the 20-skewer MAX and the modular PRO in 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 or 140 skewers. Both commercial ranges use a USB power bank only and must be used outdoors or in a genuinely well-ventilated area.

How do FM, MAX and PRO differ?

FM is the 12-skewer household model with 2 power-supply options. MAX is the commercial 20-skewer model. PRO is the modular commercial range in 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 or 140-skewer configurations. MAX and PRO use a USB power bank only. The shell warranty is one year and the motor warranty is six months from the bill of lading date.

What are the disadvantages of automatic charcoal grills?

They still burn charcoal, so they need outdoor or genuinely well-ventilated siting, and they produce carbon monoxide. Larger configurations are heavy and have to be planned into the kitchen; the exact weight is confirmed per configuration in the quotation. The external WQ157 motor is a replaceable wear part, and these grills are nearly smokeless, not smoke-free.

Is a rotating skewer grill worth it for a restaurant?

It depends on your volume. If staff turn skewers by hand through peak service, motorised rotation removes that repetitive job and cooks a full load evenly at constant speed. If covers are low, the extra weight, cleaning and charcoal handling may not pay back. Model the numbers on your own menu before ordering.

How do I estimate how many skewers one grill can serve in a shift?

Work out skewers per load multiplied by the number of loads, then subtract loading, unloading and charcoal time. A full load of standard meat skewers cooks in about 5 to 6 minutes; thicker cuts and bone-in pieces take longer. Base the calculation on your own service pattern instead of a fixed hourly figure.

Safe use

Can I use a smokeless charcoal grill indoors?

No. These grills burn charcoal and produce carbon monoxide, a colourless and odourless gas that can be fatal. Use the grill only outdoors or in a well-ventilated area, never in a closed room, tent, caravan or basement, and never to heat a living space. An open window is not enough ventilation.

Is it safe to grill indoors with charcoal if the window is open?

No. Burning charcoal produces carbon monoxide, which has no colour or smell and can kill, and an open window does not remove the risk. Use the grill outdoors on a patio or in a garden, or in a space with real mechanical ventilation, never in a closed room, tent or caravan.

How should an outdoor commercial charcoal grill station be ventilated?

Plan the station outdoors or in a genuinely well-ventilated area, never in a closed room, tent, garage or caravan. Charcoal produces carbon monoxide, which is colourless and odourless. Keep the unit clear of walls and combustible materials, and follow the local ventilation rules for your site, which are buyer-specific.
