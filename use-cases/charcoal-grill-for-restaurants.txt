}4:T26bd,<p class="geo-article__lead">A commercial charcoal grill for restaurant service must fit the way the kitchen actually works. The useful starting point is not restaurant seating alone. It is the number of BBQ orders expected during peak service, the skewers in each order, the service window, the number of cooking rounds and the staff available to load, unload and clean the grill.</p>
<p>Blue Carbon supplies charcoal-fired automatic rotating grills in two commercial ranges. MAX holds up to 20 skewers. PRO is a modular range supplied in 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 or 140-skewer configurations. The correct model depends on the restaurant workflow and must be confirmed in the quotation.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-choose-a-charcoal-grill-for-your-restaurant">How to Choose a Charcoal Grill for Your Restaurant</h2>
<p>Start with the busiest realistic service, not an average day. A grill that is adequate for a quiet lunch can become a bottleneck when several tables order skewers during the same dinner period.</p>
<p>Define these points before comparing models:</p>
<ul><li>Expected number of BBQ orders during peak service.</li><li>Average skewers per order.</li><li>Length of the main service window.</li><li>Number of complete grill rounds the kitchen can realistically serve.</li><li>Available operating area and access for loading and cleaning.</li><li>Charcoal storage, lighting and ash handling.</li><li>Outdoor or genuinely well-ventilated installation conditions.</li><li>Cleaning workflow between batches and at closing.</li><li>Staff available to operate the grill.</li><li>Required skewer capacity and product configuration.</li></ul>
<p>These inputs determine whether a compact commercial unit or a larger modular configuration is appropriate. They also help the supplier recommend a configuration without relying on a fixed covers-to-skewers assumption.</p>
<h2 id="choose-the-right-skewer-capacity">Choose the Right Skewer Capacity</h2>

<p>The MAX and PRO share the same automatic rotating skewer principle. The main difference is capacity and operating footprint. A restaurant should compare the expected peak batch with the number of rounds it can complete during the service window.</p>
<p>The PRO range is built from 20-skewer modules. Buyers should not assume that a particular number of restaurant covers automatically requires an equal number of skewers. Orders per table, menu mix and the number of serving rounds can change the calculation substantially.</p>

<h2 id="restaurant-capacity-planning">Restaurant Capacity Planning</h2>
<p>Use this buyer-side sequence:</p>
<ol><li>Estimate the peak number of BBQ orders.</li><li>Multiply by the usual skewers per order.</li><li>Define the main service window.</li><li>Decide how many complete rounds the kitchen can serve.</li><li>Compare the required batch with MAX and PRO capacities.</li><li>Confirm available space, ventilation and cleaning workflow.</li><li>Request a commercial configuration quotation.</li></ol>
<p>This is a planning framework, not a guaranteed throughput calculation. Cooking time depends on the food, and staff performance depends on loading, unloading, charcoal management and service layout. Blue Carbon should confirm the final configuration against the buyer's own assumptions.</p>
<h2 id="low-smoke-charcoal-operation">Low-Smoke Charcoal Operation</h2>
<p>The grills use real charcoal and an automatic rotating skewer system. The separate-chamber design is intended to reduce visible smoke produced when fat drips onto the fuel.</p>
<p>Reduced-smoke operation does not mean zero smoke or unrestricted indoor use. A charcoal grill produces carbon monoxide and must be used outdoors or in a genuinely well-ventilated area. Ventilation, fire safety and local installation requirements must be reviewed for the actual restaurant site.</p>
<h2 id="built-for-commercial-restaurant-workflow">Built for Commercial Restaurant Workflow</h2>
<p>The commercial range is designed around repeatable skewer cooking and a service workflow rather than a single household batch.</p>
<ul><li>Motorised skewer rotation reduces the need for staff to turn each skewer by hand.</li><li>MAX supports up to 20 skewers per cycle.</li><li>PRO supports modular configurations from 40 to 140 skewers.</li><li>Body, grates, oil tray and skewers use 304 stainless steel; the charcoal box uses 201 stainless steel.</li><li>The charcoal box, water tray and rotating shaft are removable for cleaning.</li><li>A full load of standard meat skewers cooks in about 5-6 minutes; thicker food needs longer.</li><li>The external WQ157 motor is replaceable, and spare motors are available on request.</li><li>MAX and PRO use a USB power bank only. No mains adapter is supplied or claimed.</li></ul>

<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3 id="what-type-of-charcoal-grill-is-suitable-for-a-restaurant">What type of charcoal grill is suitable for a restaurant?</h3>
<p>A commercial charcoal grill should match peak order demand, service rounds, available space, ventilation and cleaning workflow. Blue Carbon offers the 20-skewer MAX and the modular PRO range from 40 to 140 skewers.</p>
<h3 id="how-do-i-choose-between-20-40-and-larger-skewer-configurations">How do I choose between 20, 40 and larger skewer configurations?</h3>
<p>Start with expected skewers per peak batch, not restaurant seating alone. If the peak batch is compact, MAX may be a starting point. If the kitchen needs a larger modular batch, compare the 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 and 140-skewer PRO configurations.</p>
<h3 id="how-many-skewers-does-a-restaurant-need">How many skewers does a restaurant need?</h3>
<p>There is no universal number. Estimate BBQ orders, skewers per order, service window, acceptable rounds, menu composition and operating workflow. Confirm the final configuration with Blue Carbon using your own assumptions.</p>
<h3 id="is-the-grill-low-smoke">Is the grill low-smoke?</h3>
<p>The separate-chamber design is intended to reduce visible smoke compared with a conventional open-fire charcoal grill. It is nearly smokeless, not smoke-free.</p>
<h3 id="can-the-grill-be-used-indoors">Can the grill be used indoors?</h3>
<p>The current charcoal grills are intended for outdoor or genuinely well-ventilated use. Do not treat low-smoke wording as indoor-safe approval. Confirm site ventilation, fire safety and local requirements before installation.</p>
<h3 id="how-should-i-plan-capacity-for-peak-service">How should I plan capacity for peak service?</h3>
<p>Use peak orders multiplied by skewers per order, then divide by the number of rounds the kitchen can complete. Add loading, unloading, charcoal handling and cleaning time before choosing a model.</p>
