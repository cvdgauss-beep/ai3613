The buyer needs to consider event duration, serving rounds, transport, setup, available operating space, ventilation, power and cleaning after service, not just the number of guests on the invitation.</p>
<p><a href="https://ai3613.com/en/about">Blue Carbon</a> supplies charcoal-fired automatic rotating grills with different commercial capacities. MAX holds up to 20 skewers. PRO is available in modular 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 and 140-skewer configurations. The suitable starting point depends on the event workflow and must be confirmed against the buyer's own service assumptions.</p>

<h2 id="choosing-a-charcoal-grill-for-catering-operations">Choosing a Charcoal Grill for Catering Operations</h2>
<p>The first question is how the event will be served. A buffet line, a plated service and a multi-round reception can use the same equipment differently.</p>
<p>Review these operating inputs:</p>
<ul><li>Event type and service format.</li><li>Expected demand during the main service period.</li><li>Total service duration.</li><li>Number of complete cooking rounds.</li><li>Skewers expected per round.</li><li>Required skewer capacity.</li><li>Transport and setup requirements.</li><li>Available outdoor or well-ventilated operating area.</li><li>Charcoal handling and refuelling workflow.</li><li>Cleaning and pack-down process.</li><li>Power-bank requirements for the rotating motor.</li></ul>
<p>For commercial MAX and PRO units, the motor uses a USB power bank only. No mains adapter is supplied or claimed.</p>
<h2 id="catering-and-buffet-capacity-planning">Catering and Buffet Capacity Planning</h2>
<p>Use a workflow-based calculation:</p>
<ol><li>Estimate the expected BBQ orders or servings.</li><li>Estimate the skewers per order.</li><li>Divide service into acceptable rounds.</li><li>Compare the largest required batch with available grill capacity.</li><li>Add loading, unloading and charcoal time.</li><li>Confirm transport, setup and cleaning time.</li><li>Request a configuration quote using the complete assumptions.</li></ol>
<p>Do not choose a configuration from guest count alone. Menu composition, order size, waiting time and the number of operating staff can change the result. Blue Carbon does not guarantee a fixed number of guests, plates or events per hour.</p>

<h2 id="low-smoke-charcoal-design">Low-Smoke Charcoal Design</h2>
<p>The grills use real charcoal and an automatic rotating skewer system. The separate-chamber design is intended to reduce visible smoke when fat drips onto the fuel.</p>
<p>Reduced-smoke operation does not mean zero smoke or unrestricted indoor use. The grill produces carbon monoxide and must be used outdoors or in a genuinely well-ventilated area. Event organisers must check ventilation, fire safety and local requirements for the actual site.</p>
<h2 id="for-hotels-caterers-and-event-operators">For Hotels, Caterers and Event Operators</h2>
<h3 id="hotels-and-buffets">Hotels and Buffets</h3>
<p>A hotel or buffet operation should match grill capacity to its service counters, refill pattern and cleaning routine. The grill should have a defined outdoor or genuinely ventilated operating position and enough space for loading and unloading.</p>
<h3 id="catering-companies">Catering Companies</h3>
<p>A catering company should compare carton size, transport method, setup time, repeat-use cleaning and spare-motor planning. MAX uses a 38 × 42 × 32 cm carton. The 40-skewer PRO configuration uses a 74 × 42 × 32 cm carton. Larger PRO configurations are quoted separately.</p>
<h3 id="event-operators">Event Operators</h3>
<p>Event operators should define service duration, expected rounds and the acceptable wait between batches. The number of units and their configuration should be confirmed from the event plan rather than a fixed people-per-grill rule.</p>
<h2 id="catering-buyer-checklist">Catering Buyer Checklist</h2>
<p>Prepare these details before requesting a quotation:</p>
<ul><li>Event type and operating format.</li><li>Event or service location.</li><li>Service duration.</li><li>Estimated guests.</li><li>Expected BBQ orders.</li><li>Skewers per order.</li><li>Number of serving rounds.</li><li>Required grill configuration.</li><li>Required number of units.</li><li>Outdoor or ventilation conditions.</li><li>Transport and setup requirements.</li><li>Destination country and shipping terms.</li><li>Cleaning and spare-parts plan.</li><li><a href="https://ai3613.com/en/charcoal-grill-oem-odm">OEM or private-label requirement</a>, if applicable.</li></ul>
<p>The <a href="https://ai3613.com/resources/guides/commercial-charcoal-skewer-grill-buying-guide">commercial buying guide</a> explains how to compare capacity, power, cleaning and supplier support.</p>
<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3 id="what-charcoal-grill-is-suitable-for-catering">What charcoal grill is suitable for catering?</h3>
<p>The suitable model depends on event duration, expected batch size, serving rounds, transport, operating space and ventilation. MAX holds up to 20 skewers; PRO is available in modular 40 to 140-skewer configurations.</p>
<h3 id="can-the-grill-be-used-for-buffet-service">Can the grill be used for buffet service?</h3>
<p>The commercial range can be considered for buffet service when the operating area and service workflow are suitable. The buyer must confirm outdoor or genuinely well-ventilated use and plan loading, unloading and cleaning.</p>
<h3 id="how-should-i-calculate-required-capacity">How should I calculate required capacity?</h3>
<p>Estimate expected orders, skewers per order and acceptable service rounds. Add loading, unloading and charcoal time. Compare the largest batch with MAX and PRO capacities.</p>
<h3 id="which-configuration-is-suitable-for-larger-events">Which configuration is suitable for larger events?</h3>
<p>PRO is the larger modular range. Start with the event duration and number of rounds, then confirm whether a 40, 60, 80, 100, 120 or 140-skewer configuration fits the plan.</p>
<h3 id="is-it-low-smoke">Is it low-smoke?</h3>
<p>The separate chamber is intended to reduce visible smoke from dripping fat. It is nearly smokeless, not smoke-free, and still burns real charcoal.</p>
<h3 id="can-hotels-use-the-grill">Can hotels use the grill?</h3>
<p>A hotel can consider the commercial range when it has a suitable outdoor or genuinely ventilated operating location and a clear cleaning and service workflow. Local installation and fire rules must be checked.</p>
<h3 id="can-i-request-multiple-units-for-an-event-operation">Can I request multiple units for an event operation?</h3>
